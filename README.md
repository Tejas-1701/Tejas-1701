### Projects

- **[Regime-Aware Factor Model](https://github.com/Tejas-1701/regime-factor-model)**: Detects bull, bear, and volatile markets with a Hidden Markov Model and switches strategy for each one. In a backtest from 2010 to 2026, it returned 18.5% a year against the index's 12.9%, with a Sharpe ratio of 1.10 against 0.90. [Live demo](https://regime-factor-model-dgetpli9nt65vnjm4q4ubz.streamlit.app/)
- **[FluForecast](https://github.com/Tejas-1701/fluforecast)**: Forecasts flu activity several weeks ahead from CDC data, with uncertainty ranges. Across 10 US regions and 5 flu seasons, its one-week-ahead error was 44% lower than the CDC baseline. [Live demo](https://fluforecast-mrfcndv2gack6b6ohre7yj.streamlit.app/)
- **[What Actually Improves Text-to-SQL?](https://github.com/Tejas-1701/text-to-sql-agent)**: An LLM agent that turns questions into SQL, runs it and fixes its own errors. I tested 7 common accuracy tricks on the BIRD benchmark with significance tests. None beat the baseline reliably, and only switching models helped (+10.5 points, p = 0.009).
- **Classroom Hand-Raise Detection**: Spots raised hands and recognizes faces live using a YOLO model on an OAK-D camera.
